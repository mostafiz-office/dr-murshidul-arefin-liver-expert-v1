Biliary Tract Surgery Services

Biliary Conditions We Treat

Expert surgical care for bile duct obstruction, cholangiocarcinoma, cholangitis, biliary strictures, and choledochal cysts — tailored to each patient's condition by Dr. Murshidul Arefin, the best biliary tract surgery specialist in Dhaka.

Bile Duct Obstruction Repair

Bile duct obstruction caused by stones, tumors, or post-surgical strictures demands expert surgical reconstruction. Dr. Arefin performs hepaticojejunostomy, choledochojejunostomy, and bile duct exploration to restore biliary drainage and prevent recurrent jaundice.

Cholangiocarcinoma Surgery

Specializing in oncologic biliary surgery, Dr. Arefin performs radical resection of cholangiocarcinoma — including hilar (Klatskin tumor), distal, and intrahepatic bile duct cancers. His approach combines R0 resection goals with vascular reconstruction when needed to maximize survival outcomes.

Cholangitis Treatment

Acute cholangitis — a life-threatening biliary infection — requires emergency biliary decompression and targeted antibiotic therapy. Dr. Arefin provides urgent surgical and endoscopic management to resolve infection, remove obstructing stones, and prevent sepsis — with ICU-grade monitoring and round-the-clock surgical readiness.

Biliary Stricture Reconstruction

Biliary strictures from previous surgery, trauma, or inflammation require meticulous reconstructive surgery. Dr. Arefin specializes in revision hepaticojejunostomy and biliary-enteric anastomosis — restoring bile flow and preventing long-term complications like recurrent cholangitis.

Choledochal Cyst Excision

Choledochal cysts are congenital bile duct dilations that carry a risk of malignant transformation. Dr. Arefin performs complete cyst excision with Roux-en-Y hepaticojejunostomy — the definitive treatment to eliminate cancer risk and restore normal biliary anatomy.
