Pancreatic Surgery Services

Pancreatic Conditions We Treat

Expert surgical care for pancreatic cancer, pancreatitis, pancreatic cysts, and periampullary tumors — tailored to each patient's condition by Dr. Murshidul Arefin, the best pancreatic surgery specialist in Dhaka.

Whipple Pancreaticoduodenectomy

The Whipple procedure is the gold-standard operation for pancreatic head tumors and periampullary cancers. Dr. Arefin performs this complex surgery with meticulous precision — removing the head of the pancreas, duodenum, gallbladder, and part of the bile duct while preserving digestive function. Furthermore, each case is discussed in a multidisciplinary tumor board for optimal outcomes.

Pancreatic Cancer Surgery

Specializing in oncologic pancreatic surgery, Dr. Arefin performs distal pancreatectomy, total pancreatectomy, and Whipple procedures for pancreatic malignancies. His approach combines R0 resection goals with evidence-based perioperative management to improve survival and quality of life. Additionally, neoadjuvant protocols are coordinated when indicated.

Acute & Chronic Pancreatitis

From necrotizing pancreatitis requiring surgical debridement to chronic pancreatitis with duct obstruction, Dr. Arefin provides comprehensive surgical management — including necrosectomy, drainage procedures, and Frey/Puestow operations. Book a consultation for expert pancreatitis care, supported by ICU-grade perioperative monitoring and nutrition optimization throughout recovery.

Pancreatic Cyst & Pseudocyst

Pancreatic cysts and pseudocysts require careful evaluation to distinguish benign from premalignant lesions. Dr. Arefin offers expert surgical drainage, cystogastrostomy, and resection when indicated — using advanced imaging and endoscopic assessment to guide treatment.
