Liver Surgery Services

Liver Conditions We Treat

Expert surgical care for liver tumors, cysts, fatty liver disease, and liver transplant — tailored to each patient's condition by Dr. Murshidul Arefin, a leading hepatobiliary surgeon in Dhaka.

Liver Tumor & Cancer Surgery

Dr. Arefin delivers precise diagnosis and advanced surgery for benign and malignant liver tumors. Using modern hepatic resection and minimally invasive techniques, he ensures optimal tumor removal while preserving healthy tissue. For related biliary care, see gallbladder & bile duct surgery.

Liver Cyst Management

Simple or complex liver cysts are carefully assessed using advanced imaging. When intervention is required, Dr. Arefin performs safe cyst excision — including fenestration or complete removal — to prevent recurrence, relieve discomfort, and restore normal liver function with lasting results.

Fatty Liver Surgical Intervention

For patients with severe or complicated fatty liver disease, Dr. Arefin combines metabolic evaluation with surgical precision to manage liver damage, prevent deterioration, and promote healthy regeneration. Therefore, long-term fatty liver treatment outcomes remain stable across follow-up. Furthermore, related issues such as pancreatitis are reviewed in the same consultation.

Liver Transplant Surgery

A cornerstone of Dr. Arefin's expertise — offering complete evaluation, donor coordination, and advanced transplant procedures for end-stage liver failure. His approach prioritises patient safety, post-transplant recovery, and a comprehensive liver transplant program recognised across Dhaka and Bangladesh.

Liver Transplant Follow-Up Care

Dr. Arefin's transplant program provides end-to-end support — from donor assessment to post-surgery rehabilitation — ensuring a seamless experience and long-term care for transplant patients. Moreover, every patient receives personalised follow-up, scheduled imaging, and immunosuppression reviews. Book an appointment for evaluation.
