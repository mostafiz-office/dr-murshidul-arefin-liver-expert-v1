Gallbladder Stone Surgery

From painful gallstones to complicated cholelithiasis, Dr. Arefin offers laparoscopic cholecystectomy — removing stones and restoring comfort through small-incision, minimally invasive procedures with faster healing. Each patient receives a thorough pre-operative workup to ensure the safest possible outcome.

Empyema Gallbladder Treatment

Empyema of the gallbladder — a serious complication involving pus accumulation — demands urgent surgical intervention. Dr. Arefin's team performs expert laparoscopic or open drainage and cholecystectomy to resolve infection, prevent sepsis, and restore biliary function. Antibiotic protocols are coordinated to optimize recovery and minimize hospital stay.

Gallbladder & Bile Duct Cancer

Specializing in oncologic hepatobiliary surgery, Dr. Arefin performs advanced resections for gallbladder and bile duct cancers, combining surgical precision with evidence-based cancer management to extend survival and improve quality of life. For related biliary conditions, see biliary tract surgery. Multidisciplinary oncology coordination ensures comprehensive post-operative follow-up.

Cholecystitis Treatment

Acute and chronic gallbladder inflammation is managed through timely diagnosis and minimally invasive surgery to relieve pain, prevent infection, and avoid recurrence. Dr. Arefin's approach includes early intervention strategies that reduce complication rates, shorten hospital stay, and accelerate return to normal activity.

Gallbladder Polyps Management

Gallbladder polyps require careful surveillance and, when indicated, surgical removal to prevent malignant transformation. Dr. Arefin provides expert evaluation using advanced imaging and performs laparoscopic cholecystectomy when polyps exceed safe thresholds.
