Treatment Process

Your Treatment Journey

A safe, minimally invasive pathway for gallbladder disease — from focused diagnosis through laparoscopic cholecystectomy and rapid, well-supported recovery.

Step 1

Consultation & Diagnosis

We start with a focused clinical assessment of your symptoms — biliary colic, indigestion, jaundice — supported by abdominal ultrasound and liver function tests, and where required MRCP to map gallstones, polyps, or inflammation precisely.

Step 3

Laparoscopic Cholecystectomy

Minimally invasive removal of the gallbladder through 3–4 keyhole incisions, using high-definition laparoscopy and meticulous Calot's triangle dissection to safely identify and protect the biliary tree throughout the procedure.
