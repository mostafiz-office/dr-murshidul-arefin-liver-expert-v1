Our Services

Comprehensive Hepatobiliary & Liver Treatment Services

Expert care for liver, gallbladder, biliary, pancreatic, and upper gastrointestinal conditions—led by Dr. Murshidul Arefin, one of Dhaka's top hepatobiliary and liver transplant surgeons.

Liver Tumor & Cancer Surgery

Dr. Arefin provides precise diagnosis and advanced surgical treatment for both benign and malignant liver tumors. Using modern resection and minimally invasive techniques, he ensures optimal tumor removal.

Liver Cyst Management

Simple or complex liver cysts are carefully assessed using advanced imaging. When surgery is required, Dr. Arefin performs safe cyst excision to prevent recurrence, relieve discomfort, and restore normal liver function.

Fatty Liver Surgical Intervention

For patients with severe or complicated fatty liver disease, Dr. Arefin combines metabolic evaluation with surgical precision to manage liver damage, prevent further deterioration, and promote healthy regeneration.

Liver Transplant Surgery

A cornerstone of Dr. Arefin's expertise—offering complete evaluation, donor coordination, and advanced transplant procedures for end-stage liver failure. His approach focuses on patient safety, post-transplant recovery, and long-term liver health.

Gallstone & Gallbladder Surgery

From painful gallstones to inflamed gallbladder conditions, Dr. Arefin offers laparoscopic cholecystectomy—removing stones and restoring comfort through small-incision, minimally invasive procedures with faster healing.

Bile Duct & Biliary Tract Surgery

Dr. Arefin treats complex bile duct obstructions and biliary cancers using precise microsurgical methods. His care ensures proper bile flow, alleviates jaundice, and minimizes postoperative complications.

Gallbladder & Bile Duct Cancer Surgery

Specializing in oncologic hepatobiliary surgery, Dr. Arefin performs advanced resections for gallbladder and bile duct cancers, combining surgical precision with evidence-based cancer management to extend survival and improve quality of life.

Cholecystitis Treatment

Acute and chronic gallbladder inflammation is managed through timely diagnosis and minimally invasive surgery to relieve pain, prevent infection, and avoid recurrence.

Pancreatic Cancer Surgery

For pancreatic tumors, Dr. Arefin performs highly specialized surgeries—including Whipple procedures—aimed at removing cancer while protecting vital structures. His multidisciplinary approach ensures optimal outcomes in challenging cases.

Pancreatic Cyst Treatment

Pancreatic cysts can pose serious risks if untreated. Dr. Arefin provides expert evaluation and surgical management, ensuring safe cyst removal and long-term digestive health.

Pancreatitis (Surgical Management)

Severe or chronic pancreatitis requiring surgery is treated with precision to relieve obstruction, prevent recurrence, and preserve pancreatic function—helping patients regain comfort and nutritional stability.

Spleen-Related Surgeries

Dr. Arefin offers splenectomy and related procedures for trauma, cysts, or hematologic disorders, ensuring safe removal with minimal blood loss and smooth recovery.

Upper Gastrointestinal (GIT) Surgery

Comprehensive management for esophageal, gastric, and duodenal disorders—combining diagnostic endoscopy with advanced surgical intervention to restore digestive health and quality of life.

Liver Transplant Coordination & Follow-Up Care

Dr. Arefin's transplant program provides end-to-end support—from donor assessment to post-surgery rehabilitation—ensuring a seamless experience and long-term care for transplant patients.
