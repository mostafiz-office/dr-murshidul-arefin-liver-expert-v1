What conditions does Dr. Arefin treat?

Dr. Arefin specialises in hepatobiliary, liver, gallbladder, biliary tract and pancreatic disease, covering both benign and malignant presentations. Consequently, complex referrals from general surgeons and physicians form a large share of weekly cases. Moreover, common conditions include liver tumours and resections, gallstones and cholecystitis, bile duct strictures and pancreatic tumours and resections. Indeed, more than a decade of focused practice supports each treatment recommendation. Notably, multidisciplinary discussion with radiology and surgical oncology is built into complex cases. Does Dr. Arefin perform laparoscopic (key-hole) surgery?

Yes — whenever clinically appropriate, procedures are performed using laparoscopic (key-hole) technique. Therefore, patients benefit from smaller incisions, less post-operative pain and faster return to normal activity. Moreover, laparoscopic gallbladder removal is performed routinely, alongside selected liver and biliary procedures. Consequently, hospital stays are typically shorter compared with traditional open surgery. However, key-hole surgery is not appropriate for every case, and the choice is always individualised. Additionally, the same surgeon performs both open and laparoscopic operations — there is no compromise on safety to fit a particular technique. Thus, decisions are based on tumour stage, anatomy and patient fitness rather than convenience. Indeed, advanced laparoscopic training underpins every procedure offered. Notably, recovery protocols are aligned with current international recovery guidelines. Ultimately, the goal remains a complete, oncologically sound operation with the least disruption possible to your life.
