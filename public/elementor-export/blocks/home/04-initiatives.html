OUR PROGRAMS

Our Initiatives

At Dr. Murshidul Arefin's Hepatobiliary & Liver Care Center, our mission extends beyond the operation theater. We're committed to empowering patients and their families through education, compassion, and continuous support.

Surgery Assistance

We offer guidance and support for patients in need of complex liver, gallbladder, and pancreatic surgeries, helping them access the right care and financial assistance for timely treatment.

Health Education

Through awareness programs and liver health seminars, Dr. Arefin and his team educate the community about early detection, lifestyle management, and prevention of liver diseases in Bangladesh.

Support Groups

Our patient network connects individuals and families who are undergoing hepatobiliary or liver transplant treatments, creating a safe space for emotional support and recovery.
