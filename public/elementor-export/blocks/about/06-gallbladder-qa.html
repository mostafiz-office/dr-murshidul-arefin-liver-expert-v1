What is gallbladder surgery in Dhanmondi, Dhaka?

Gallbladder surgery at Dr. Arefin's Dhanmondi chamber most often means a laparoscopic cholecystectomy — a keyhole removal of the gallbladder, typically performed for symptomatic gallstones. Therefore, most patients go home within 24 hours and return to light activity inside a week. Moreover, more than 1,000 hepatobiliary procedures inform the operative protocol used here. Consequently, complication rates remain low while pain control and scar size both stay minimal.

When does Dr. Arefin recommend gallbladder removal over watchful waiting?

Removal is recommended when gallstones cause repeated pain, cholecystitis, jaundice or pancreatitis — or when imaging shows a high-risk gallbladder. However, asymptomatic stones in low-risk patients are often safely monitored. Therefore, every recommendation here is individualized: a second-opinion review on outside reports is welcomed before surgery is booked. Moreover, alternative care paths are explained openly, not just the operative one.

Is laparoscopic gallbladder surgery safe for elderly patients?

Yes — with proper anaesthetic and cardiac assessment, laparoscopic cholecystectomy is the safest option for most elderly patients with symptomatic stones. Therefore, age alone is rarely the deciding factor; overall fitness and comorbidities matter more. Moreover, recovery is faster than open surgery, which reduces hospital stay and pneumonia risk.

How long is recovery after gallbladder surgery in Dhaka?

Most patients leave hospital within 24 hours and resume desk work or light routine inside 5–7 days. Therefore, the practical recovery window is short for almost everyone undergoing elective gallbladder surgery. However, heavy lifting and strenuous exercise should wait 3–4 weeks. Moreover, dietary changes are usually modest — most patients return to a normal diet after a brief low-fat transition.
