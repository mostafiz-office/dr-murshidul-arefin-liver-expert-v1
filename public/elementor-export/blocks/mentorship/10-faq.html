Frequently Asked Questions

Who is eligible for the mentorship program?

The program is open to MBBS students, intern doctors, postgraduate surgical trainees, faculty members, and healthcare professionals who wish to develop or advance their skills in hepatobiliary and liver transplant surgery. No prior hepatobiliary experience is required for entry-level tracks.

How long does the mentorship program last?

Program duration varies by track. Observership modules run for 2–4 weeks, while comprehensive surgical training tracks span 3–6 months depending on the participant's level and goals. Custom durations can be arranged for visiting international fellows.

What are the steps to apply?

Applying is straightforward: 1) Submit your application through the contact form on this website. 2) Attach your CV, medical credentials, and a brief statement of interest. 3) The team will review your profile and schedule a short interview or orientation call within 7 working days.

What criteria are used for selection?

Selection is based on academic qualifications, clinical experience, motivation for hepatobiliary surgery, and professional references. Priority is given to candidates demonstrating genuine commitment to surgical excellence and those from underserved regions seeking advanced training.

Is there a fee for the mentorship program?

Some tracks may involve a nominal administrative fee to cover materials and facility access. However, Dr. Arefin's mentorship philosophy prioritizes accessibility — financial assistance and fee waivers are available for deserving candidates on a case-by-case basis.

Will I receive a certificate upon completion?

Yes. All participants who successfully complete the program receive a certificate of completion endorsed by Dr. Murshidul Arefin. Exceptional trainees may also receive recommendation letters to support their career advancement.
