Who Can Join

Our mentorship and training programs are open to MBBS students, intern doctors, postgraduate trainees, faculty members, and healthcare professionals eager to enhance their surgical skills. Whether you're beginning your journey or advancing your expertise in hepatobiliary surgery, Dr. Arefin's hands-on guidance helps you grow with confidence and precision.

MBBS Students

Foundational hepatobiliary knowledge via seminars and liver health awareness programs.

Intern Doctors

Hands-on surgical, operation-theatre, and patient-management experience under expert mentorship.

Postgraduate

Advanced training in liver resections, biliary reconstruction, and transplant coordination.

Professionals

Specialised hepatobiliary care, perioperative management, and structured clinical training.
