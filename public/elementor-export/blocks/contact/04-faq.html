Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly will I receive confirmation after submitting the appointment form?

Our team usually confirms appointment requests within 24 hours on working days, often sooner. You will receive a confirmation by phone call, SMS, or WhatsApp on +880 1311 487 592, along with your chamber slot at Popular Medical College Hospital, Dhanmondi. For same-day or urgent hepatobiliary consultations, please call us directly instead of using the form.

Can patients from outside Dhaka or abroad book a consultation with Dr. Arefin?

Yes. Dr. Arefin regularly consults patients from across Bangladesh as well as international patients from India, Nepal, the Middle East, and beyond. We offer WhatsApp video consultations, second-opinion reviews of imaging and biopsy reports, and pre-travel planning so you arrive in Dhaka with a clear liver, gallbladder, bile duct, or pancreatic surgery roadmap.

What medical reports and documents should I bring to my first chamber visit?

Please bring all previous prescriptions, discharge summaries, recent blood tests (LFT, CBC, coagulation profile), and any ultrasound, CT, MRI, MRCP or PET-CT scans related to your liver, gallbladder, bile duct or pancreas. If you have biopsy slides or histopathology reports, bring those too. Soft copies on a USB drive or phone are equally welcome — Dr. Arefin reviews everything during your hepatobiliary consultation.

Is parking available at Popular Medical College Hospital and which entrance leads to Dr. Arefin's chamber?

Yes — Popular Medical College Hospital, Dhanmondi-02 offers on-site patient parking. Dr. Arefin sees patients in Room-304 (Building-06) and Room-302 (Building-03). The Building-06 entrance is the most convenient — take the lift to the third floor and follow signs for the Hepatobiliary & Liver Transplant Chamber. Wheelchair access and lift support are available for elderly or post-operative patients.

Can I reschedule or cancel an appointment, and are there any charges?

Yes — you can reschedule or cancel free of charge by calling or messaging +880 1311 487 592 at least 4 hours before your slot. We will offer the next available chamber time that suits you. Repeated last-minute cancellations may delay future bookings during peak evening hours (5–9 PM), so early notice helps us serve other patients waiting for hepatobiliary consultation.
