## Bengali keyword usage strategy

> **Approach: Same site, Bengali meta + structured data + content blocks**
>
> Bengali keywords are integrated into the *existing English site* via:
> 1. **Image `alt` text** — every asset image includes a Bengali keyword variant in alt text
> 2. **Meta descriptions** — pages targeting gallbladder surgery include Bengali in `<meta name="description">`
> 3. **JSON-LD `description`** — Physician schema includes Bengali name/specialty variants
> 4. **FAQ sections** — Bengali questions rendered as visible `<details>`/`<summary>` or FAQ blocks on service pages (also marked up with `FAQPage` schema)
> 5. **`hreflang`** — set `<link rel="alternate" hreflang="bn" href="...">` pointing to the same page (signals Bengali content to Google)
>
> This avoids maintaining a separate Bangla site while capturing Bengali search traffic.
