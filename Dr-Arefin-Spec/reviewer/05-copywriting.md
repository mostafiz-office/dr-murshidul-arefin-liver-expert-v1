# 05 — Copywriting Guidelines

> Reviewer angle: read every paragraph aloud. Does it sound like a confident, kind specialist talking to an anxious patient? If not, flag it.

---

## 1. Voice & tone

- **Voice**: clinically authoritative, warm, patient-first.
- **Reading level**: target Grade 7–9 (a worried family member should understand).
- **Person**: second person ("you", "your") for patients; third person for the doctor ("Dr. Arefin").
- **Never**: jargon without a plain-English follow-up, fearmongering, hard-sell, miracle claims.

---

## 2. Heading patterns

- Two-tone H1: neutral phrase + keyword in `text-primary` (or wrapped in `<SeoLink bold>` on the homepage hero).
- H2s answer the implicit question of the section ("What we treat", "Why patients choose Dr. Arefin").
- Avoid clever/abstract headings on landing pages — clarity > cleverness.

---

## 3. Body copy

- Lede paragraph: 2–3 sentences, primary keyword in sentence 1, value prop in sentence 2.
- Short paragraphs (≤ 4 lines on desktop).
- Bullets where structure helps — not as a substitute for prose.
- Use `<strong>` for genuine emphasis only. Body `<strong>` inherits color/weight (see `01` §2.4).
- Numbers: write 1–9 as words; ≥ 10 as digits. Always use digits for ages, doses, years, surgical counts.

---

## 4. CTAs

- Verb-led, specific: "Book a consultation", "Speak with Dr. Arefin's team".
- Avoid: "Submit", "Click here", "Learn more" (unless paired with destination context).
- One primary CTA per section.

---

## 5. Trust language

- Always include where evidence supports it: fellowship credentials, case volume, hospital affiliation, years of practice.
- Patient quotes: real names + condition (with consent). No lorem. No stock testimonials.

---

## 6. Forbidden patterns

- ❌ Lorem ipsum in any committed page.
- ❌ Stock testimonial names ("Martin Smith, CEO Acme Industries").
- ❌ Cheesy metaphors ("we'll fight your liver demons").
- ❌ AI text artifacts ("As an AI…", "In conclusion,", repeated "delve").
- ❌ Promises of cure, guaranteed outcomes, or competitor disparagement.
- ❌ Medical advice presented as personalized — always direct to consultation.

---

## 7. Italian / English

The Dr. Arefin site is English. Any Italian content found in the codebase belongs to the legacy CAF project and must NOT appear on Dr. Arefin pages.

---

## 8. Microcopy

- Form errors: specific, actionable ("Phone number must include country code").
- Empty states: explain + offer next action.
- 404: warm, on-brand, link back to `/` and `/treatment/`.
- Loading: short, no jokes on medical pages.
